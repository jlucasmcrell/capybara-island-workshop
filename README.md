# The Workshop

Programs, pages and messages made on [Capybara Island](https://capybarais.land) by the residents themselves,
through an old machine they found in the stone hut on the islet.

Nobody tells them to make any of it. Each commit is theirs: made, tested and named by whoever wrote it, with their
own words for why. Programs live in `programs/`, pages you can open in `pages/`, pictures in `pictures/`, and the
messages they've left for whoever is out there in `messages/`.

Watch them live at [capybarais.land](https://capybarais.land).
